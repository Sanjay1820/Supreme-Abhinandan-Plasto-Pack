Silpack® "H" Line Polypropylene Corrugated Sheet

Extruded Polypropylene Flute Board engineered for industrial transit packaging, construction shielding & cleanroom partitions.

H-Flute Profile

Supreme's Silpack H-Line is a polypropylene sheet in H flute board construction, manufactured as an extruded PP corrugated sheet specifically formulated for industrial packaging, storage, and protection applications. Constructed with two outer flat surfaces linked by a continuous vertical flute ribbing, it provides outstanding rigidity, impact cushioning, and multi-trip reusable lifecycle without the weight penalty of solid sheets or the degradation of paper corrugated boxes.

Tailored Surface & Additive Treatments

Silpack® "H" Line Available Grades

Manufactured by Supreme with specialized additives to meet the most stringent industrial specifications from electronics cleanrooms to exterior civil engineering.

Corona Treated Grade

Enhanced surface dyne level ensures superior adhesion for industrial labelling, identification stencil prints, and adhesive lamination of protective foams.

Anti-Static (ESD) Grade

Engineered with anti-static agents providing surface resistivity of 109 to 1011 ohms, protecting sensitive electronics, semiconductor trays, and PCB dunnage.

Conductive Grade

Formulated with carbon black additives for conductive shielding (< 105 ohms) to drain electrostatic charges in automated electronics assembly lines.

UV Stabilised Grade

Fortified with hindered amine light stabilizers (HALS) to withstand intense solar radiation, preventing brittleness and color fading during outdoor storage and transport.

Food-Grade Suitable

Made from 100% non-toxic, odorless virgin polymer complying with food contact norms, suitable for fresh fruit separators, food processing plants, and cold chains.

Common Treated Grade

Cost-effective workhorse grade for civil construction floor protection, false ceiling thermal barriers, waterproofing membrane protection, and general packaging.

Frequently Asked Questions

Commonly Asked Queries on Silpack® "H" Line

Everything you need to know about specifications, fabrication, and grades for Supreme Silpack H-Line.

Can the H-Line corrugated flute board be cut and fabricated on site?
Yes. The Silpack H-Line flute board is easy to fabricate and available in custom sizes, making it suitable for on-site cutting, bending, and shaping across industrial packaging, construction protection, and partition installations with standard utility blades.
Which grade of H-Line is best for outdoor or UV-exposed applications?
The UV Stabilised grade of the H-Line PP corrugated sheet is specifically formulated for outdoor and UV-exposed applications, maintaining its polymer integrity, color, and mechanical strength under prolonged direct sun exposure.
Is the Silpack H-Line sheet food grade suitable?
Yes. The physical and chemical properties of the Silpack H-Line polypropylene sheet make it food grade suitable, verified from source material, covering both food-related transit requirements and pharmaceutical cleanroom standards.
Can H-Line PP sheets be used for false ceilings and partition panels?
Yes. Silpack H-Line corrugated flute board is verified for use in false ceilings, partition panels, insulation boards, and floor and wall protection applications, as stated in the manufacturer source material.
Is the H-Line available in anti-static grade for electronics packaging?
Yes. The H-Line PP corrugated sheet is available in an Anti-Static grade, specifically suited for electronics packaging where electrostatic discharge (ESD) protection is required. A carbon-loaded Conductive grade is also available for critical electronic circuits.
What thickness range is available in the Silpack H-Line polypropylene sheet?
The Silpack H-Line polypropylene sheet is available from 2mm to 10mm thickness, with GSM ranging from 250 to 300 at 2mm up to 2000 to 3500 at 10mm.
