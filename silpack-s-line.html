S-Flute Profile

Supreme's Silpack S-Line is a polypropylene plastic sheet in S flute board construction, manufactured as an extruded PP corrugated sheet specifically suited to advertising, printing, stationery, and point-of-sale display applications. This fluted plastic board shares the same premium base material quality as the H-Line but is optimized for print-surface flatness and visual display requirements rather than heavy industrial transit.

Material Selection Guide

How Is Silpack® "S" Line Different from "H" Line?

Understanding the right flute geometry for your specific project requirements.

Silpack "S" Line

Optimized for Printing & Visual Displays

Features an "S" flute profile engineered to provide the smoothest possible outer skin surface. Ink flows uniformly without banding or flute lines, producing photo-realistic digital prints, vibrant screen printing, and smooth vinyl wraps.

  • Flute Profile: S-Flute uniform surface
  • Core Focus: Graphic adhesion, smooth finish, POS aesthetic
  • Best Uses: Advertising signboards, retail stands, brochures
Silpack "H" Line

Engineered for Heavy Industrial Packaging

Features vertical connecting ribs between twin walls to maximize compressive loading and vertical crush resistance. Ideal for repeated multi-trip transit boxes, tier stacking dunnage, partition plates, and building protection.

  • Flute Profile: Vertical H-Flute structural ribs
  • Core Focus: Compression strength, drop impact, ESD options
  • Best Uses: Reusable transit dunnage, floor guards, partitions

Frequently Asked Questions

Commonly Asked Queries on Silpack® "S" Line

Clarifications on print capabilities, moisture resistance, and customization options for Supreme Silpack S-Line.

How is the S-Line different from the H-Line polypropylene sheet?
The S-Line fluted plastic board shares the same base PP material as the H-Line but is optimised for printing, advertising, and display applications. The H-Line is built for heavy industrial use such as packaging, partitions, and structural applications.
What colours are available in the Silpack S-Line range?
Colours are available as per customer requirements. The S-Line polypropylene sheet is not limited to standard colour options, allowing full alignment with corporate branding and retail aesthetics.
Does the S-Line sheet hold up against moisture for retail and outdoor use?
Yes. Silpack S-Line is waterproof and moisture resistant as verified from source material, maintaining its structure and print quality in humid retail and challenging outdoor environments without delaminating or warping.
Is the S-Line suited for outdoor advertising boards and display stands?
Yes. The S-Line PP corrugated sheet is waterproof, moisture resistant, and lightweight, making it suited for outdoor advertising boards, road signages, and retail display applications.
Can S-Line polypropylene sheets be cut and fabricated into custom signage sizes?
Yes. The S-Line fluted plastic board is easy to fabricate and available in customised length and width up to 1500mm, covering custom signage, POS display, and box packaging size requirements.
