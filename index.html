Rigorous Quality Assurance

Validated for Extreme Industrial Environments

Every Supreme pallet, crate, and wastebin undergoes destructive mechanical stress testing to ensure zero failure in demanding operational supply chains.

01

Load Deflection Test

Hydraulic load testing up to 6000 kg static and 1200 kg dynamic racking load to guarantee zero deflection across beam racks.

02

Sub-Zero Drop Impact

Free-fall corner and edge drop impact verification at -20°C in refrigerated testing chambers ensuring zero brittle fracture.

03

ESCR Chemical Test

Environmental Stress Crack Resistance (ASTM D1693) validation against corrosive acids, industrial alkalis, and oils.

04

Cleanroom & ISPM-15

100% exempt from phytosanitary quarantine and heat treatments, eliminating insect, pest, and fungal import hurdles.

05

EN 840 Durability

Automated tipper trunnion lifting and wheel fatigue life tests conforming to European EN 840 municipal refuse standards.
