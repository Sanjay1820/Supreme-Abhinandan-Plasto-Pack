Engineering Excellence

Why Industry Leaders Choose Supreme Roto Crates

Supreme rotationally moulded crates are manufactured using computerized machinery to ensure consistent wall thickness, high impact resistance, and zero structural seams.

Single-Piece Seamless

Bi-axial rotational moulding eliminates weak weld lines, glue joints, and seams, guaranteeing 100% leak-proof performance.

Ribs For High Strength

High-aspect ratio vertical sidewall corrugations withstand massive lateral loads and prevent outward bulging when stacked high.

Chemical & Food Grade

100% virgin polymer compliant with FDA food standards. Impervious to caustic chemicals, industrial oils, dyes, and acids.

Ergonomic Handling

Engineered built-in recessed grips or heavy galvanized steel drop handles ensure worker safety and fatigue-free manual lifting.
