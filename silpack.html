Industrial Grade

Silpack® "H" Line

Constructed with vertical "H" flute connecting ribs between two solid PP skins. Engineered for maximum impact resistance, heavy compression, reusable packaging dunnage, floor protection, and cleanroom partitions.

Thickness: 2mm – 10mm
GSM: 250 – 3500
Max Width: 1500 mm
Grades: Corona, Anti-Static, Conductive, UV

Print & Advertising Grade

Silpack® "S" Line

Engineered with "S" flute profile to deliver exceptional outer skin smoothness. Eliminates flute shadows for razor-sharp UV flatbed, digital, and screen printing across outdoor advertising, POS stands, and stationery.

Thickness: 2mm – 10mm

Across Key Industries

Where Is Silpack® Polypropylene Used?

Silpack replaces wood, corrugated paper, and heavy plastics with lightweight, washable, and 100% circular solutions.

Industrial Packaging & Dunnage

Reusable transit boxes, collapsible sleeves, component divider partitions, and interlayer separator plates that survive 50+ logistics trips.

Advertising & Retail POS

Large-format signages, election hoardings, freestanding retail display stands, trade show kiosks, and promotional product dispensers.

Construction & Interiors

Floor and tile protection during interior fitouts, false ceiling tiles, cleanroom wall linings, and waterproofing membrane backing boards.

Automotive & Electronics

Anti-static and conductive tote trays, PCB separator racks, instrument cluster transit boxes, and shock-absorbing dunnage.

Stationery & Education

Presentation ring binders, document storage files, clipboards, portfolio cases, and lightweight indestructible book sleeves.

Food & Agriculture

100% food-grade fruit separator trays, hydro-cooling vegetable shippers, seafood packaging, and cold-storage thermal dunnage.
